1. Introduction

Welcome to WordyGrid, a word block puzzle game developed by MaraQueens Corp ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of WordyGrid. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our game.

2. Information We Collect

WordyGrid is designed to be enjoyed without creating a personal account or submitting personal identification information.

  • Local Save Data: Your level progress, high scores, coins, and power-up inventory are stored locally on your device storage. We do not transmit or store your personal gameplay data on our external servers.
  • Device Identifiers & Technical Data: Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may automatically collect anonymized technical data, such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, and general usage statistics to serve advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

WordyGrid integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect and use non-personal advertising identifiers to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's advertising policies.

For more information on how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. In-App Purchases

WordyGrid offers optional in-app purchases for virtual items (such as coin packs and booster bundles). All financial transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Store Billing API or Apple App Store. MaraQueens Corp does not collect, store, or have access to your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

5. Children's Privacy

WordyGrid does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at maraqueenscorp@gmail.com so we can delete the information prompt.

6. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can manage or reset your device Advertising ID and ad personalization preferences at any time through your Android device settings under Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
